GKF-WA

WhatsApp AI Lead Management for Fitness Businesses

A conversational lead management and qualification solution developed, hosted, and operated by Interroot Labs, designed specifically for gyms, health clubs, and fitness studios.

What It Is & Why It Exists

GKF-WA is a dedicated WhatsApp conversational assistant and lead management platform developed by Interroot Labs. For fitness businesses, the vast majority of prospective member inquiries arrive via WhatsApp asking about facility timings, trial sessions, personal training, and membership pricing.

When gym staff are busy coaching on the floor or managing the front desk, prospective members often wait hours for simple answers. GKF-WA solves this by delivering instant, verified responses, holding natural conversations to discover prospect fitness goals, structuring lead profiles, and flagging conversations for human staff when personal attention or trial bookings are needed.

Product At A Glance
  • ✓ Service Provider: Interroot Labs
  • ✓ Target Industry: Gyms, fitness centers, health & athletic clubs
  • ✓ Primary Channel: Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API
  • ✓ Architecture: Multi-tenant isolation, SQLite CRM, durable queueing
  • ✓ Flagship Deployment: Goldkraft Fitness (Bangalore)

Meta Platform • Data Transparency & Review Notice

Customer & Platform Data Usage

This public specification explains in plain, non-technical language exactly what customer and WhatsApp Platform Data is received by GKF-WA and why it is used.

1

What Data Is Used

WhatsApp Contact Information

The customer’s WhatsApp phone number (WhatsApp ID / wa_id) and their public WhatsApp profile display name (when provided in the incoming message metadata).

Customer Messages & Conversation History

The text content of incoming questions or replies sent by the customer, along with message timestamps to maintain coherent dialogue context.

Information Shared Voluntarily by Customers

Any preferences the customer explicitly shares during the conversation, such as primary fitness goals (e.g., strength training, CrossFit, weight loss), prior gym experience, preferred workout timings, or trial visit availability.

2

Why It Is Used

→
To Respond to Customer Inquiries

To provide instant, accurate answers about gym operating hours, address, equipment, membership packages, and trial session guidelines.

→
To Understand Customer Interests

To recognize what kind of training the prospect is looking for (general access, 1-on-1 coaching, or group workouts) and provide relevant guidance.

→
To Organize and Qualify Leads

To structure prospect records in the gym's CRM so business management can view which leads are new, qualified, or ready to join.

→
To Equip Staff for Personal Follow-up

To give gym trainers and managers conversation context so follow-up calls or messages are well-informed, personal, and helpful.

→
To Identify Needs for Human Assistance

To instantly detect when a customer asks to speak with a human coach, requests trial bookings, has injury concerns, or submits a facility complaint.

Strict Business Isolation

Each fitness business operates within an isolated tenant partition. Customer records, lead dossiers, and messages are never mixed or shared across businesses.

No Commercial Resale

Customer data is solely used to facilitate communication between the gym and its prospect. Data is never sold, rented, monetized, or shared with third-party advertisers.

PII Protection in Diagnostics

Diagnostic application logs automatically mask phone numbers (e.g. 91*****3210) and sensitive payloads to preserve user confidentiality.

Safety & Human-in-the-Loop

Responsible AI Guardrails

GKF-WA is engineered with concrete architectural boundaries to guarantee customer safety and ensure staff remain the final decision-makers.

HUMAN STAFF HANDOFF

Conversations are automatically flagged for staff intervention whenever a customer explicitly asks for a human, requests a trial workout date, inquires about corporate or custom pricing, or submits feedback.

Categorized Handoff Reasons

MEDICAL & INJURY FILTER

If a prospect inquires about spinal issues, disc herniation, ligament tears, cardiac conditions, or post-surgery rehab, GKF-WA immediately issues a safety disclaimer stating that medical advice is never given over chat, and alerts senior coaches directly.

Proactive Health Disclaimer

CONVERSION PROTECTION

The system enforces strict operational policy: the automated conversational assistant is prevented from marking leads as "converted". A customer status can only be updated to converted by authorized gym staff upon verified enrollment.
